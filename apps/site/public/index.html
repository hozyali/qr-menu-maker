A menu that fits Pakistan

Most printed menus are wrong the week after they come back from the press. With TomatoFry, you change a price once and every table has it.

  • Half and full. One karahi can have two prices, or small, medium and large for a pizza.
  • Urdu or English. Write each dish the way your customers read it.
  • Light on cheap phones. The menu is built to open quickly on weak internet, with or without food photos.
  • Call, WhatsApp, Map. One tap reaches your restaurant from the menu.

Sold out at 9pm?

Mark a dish as not available today with one click from your phone. Customers stop asking for it, and it returns when you turn it back on.

You do not need a designer, a printer, or a new QR code. Your menu lives in one place, and you are the only one who can edit it.

Short answers

Does the QR code change when I edit my menu? No. It points to your restaurant's link, and that link never changes.

Do customers need an app? No. They scan with the phone's camera and the menu opens in the browser. They do not log in.

Can I write the menu in Urdu? Yes. Dish names and descriptions can be in Urdu or English.

I have no food photos. Is that a problem? No. Photos are optional, and the menu is designed to read well without them.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro? Your menu stays online and your QR code keeps working. You just cannot add new dishes over the Free limit.

Can customers order or pay through it? Not now. TomatoFry shows your menu. Customers can call or WhatsApp you from it.

Can another restaurant see or change my menu? They cannot change it. Your account only reaches your own restaurant, and your menu page is public for customers.

Can I start today? Not yet. TomatoFry is being built in public, and signup opens soon.
